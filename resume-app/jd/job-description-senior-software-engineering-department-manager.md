## Role overview

LASP seeks a department manager to lead its 63-person Software Engineering Department through functional leads. The department combines software groups supporting flight software, mission operations, data systems, and web applications. The manager will help bring these disciplines together through shared engineering practices, tools, technical authority, and collaboration across the mission lifecycle. The position reports to the Deputy Director for Technology and participates in the Technology Division leadership team.

## Key responsibilities

- Develop functional leads as technical and people managers, and guide their hiring, development, and evaluation of engineers and student employees.
- Set department-wide performance expectations, manage outcomes, and foster a healthy and productive culture.
- Guide technical direction for spacecraft and instrument flight software and mission ground software, including command and control, planning, science-data processing, telemetry, web applications, support equipment, and simulation.
- Own software development and verification processes, tailoring them to mission classification, sponsor expectations, and project scope.
- Modernize design, build, and verification practices, including responsible use of AI-assisted development.
- Establish software strategy, roadmaps, and plans for reuse across LASP.
- Contribute software approaches, staffing, and cost estimates to proposals and new mission planning.
- Partner with project managers, mission assurance, and scientists in a matrixed organization.

## Required qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in computer science, engineering, or a related field.
- At least 12 years of software engineering experience, including at least 6 years in space-mission embedded flight software or ground software supporting mission operations or science-data processing.
- At least 5 years supervising software engineers, including hiring, evaluation, and staff development.

## Additional required experience and capabilities

- Software leadership or systems-engineering experience on space missions or instruments.
- Hands-on expertise in flight or mission ground software and working knowledge of the other.
- Understanding of mission lifecycle milestones and sponsor standards, such as NASA NPR 7150.2.
- Modern software engineering practices—including CI, automated tests, static analysis, and incremental delivery—in a milestone-driven lifecycle.
- Strong communication with engineers, scientists, project managers, and sponsors.

## Preferred qualifications

- Managing managers or technical leads.
- Delivering software across a full mission lifecycle, from requirements through launch and operations.
- Maturing engineering practices and standardizing toolchains across teams.
- Experience in a science-focused organization.
