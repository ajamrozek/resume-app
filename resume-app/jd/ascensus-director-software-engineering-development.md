# Director, Software Engineering & Development

- **Company:** Ascensus
- **Location:** Remote, Pennsylvania
- **Employment type:** Full time
- **Posted:** September 23, 2026
- **Source:** [Ascensus careers](https://careers.ascensus.com/jobs/director-software-engineering-development-remote-pennsylvania-united-states-6b30e388-8008-4582-8cda-d347a104f074)
- **Base salary range:** $170,000–$220,000, plus potential bonus and benefits

## Role summary

Lead a cross-functional team delivering and operating Ascensus's Enterprise AI Platform. This hands-on people-management role combines team development, technical guidance, delivery ownership, and improvements to engineering practices. The team may include software engineers, SDETs, DevOps engineers, and support engineers.

## Responsibilities

- Coach and develop team members through feedback, goal setting, performance discussions, and career planning.
- Build a high-performing culture centered on ownership, accountability, trust, and continuous improvement.
- Guide delivery of enterprise AI platform capabilities while balancing speed, quality, security, reliability, and maintainability.
- Stay technically engaged in architecture, code, delivery workflows, and operations; contribute directly when needed to unblock work or address critical issues.
- Improve engineering, testing, DevOps, production support, and operational practices.
- Work with product, architecture, AI, business, and technology stakeholders to turn platform goals into team execution.
- Support hiring, team growth, and development of team capabilities.

## Required qualifications

- At least 5 years of professional software engineering or related technical experience.
- At least 2 years of technical, team, people, or engineering-management leadership experience.
- Experience coaching or mentoring engineers or related technical staff.
- Ability to engage with code, architecture, development workflows, and operational tooling, including occasional hands-on work.
- Familiarity with modern software practices such as clean code, source control, unit testing, CI/CD, refactoring, maintainability, and production support.
- Experience supporting production systems, including troubleshooting, incident response, root-cause analysis, or operational improvements.
- Knowledge of Agile delivery and experience developing talent, managing priorities, communicating across technical and non-technical groups, and partnering across functions.
- Bachelor's degree in a related field or equivalent practical experience.

## Preferred experience

- Leading enterprise, internal developer, AI, or shared-services platforms.
- AI-enabled applications or engineering practices, including LLMs, RAG, chatbots, agent workflows, or AI coding tools.
- Azure, Azure AI Foundry, Azure DevOps, or other Microsoft cloud technologies.
- Automated testing, SDET practices, Playwright, Python test automation, or end-to-end testing.
- CI/CD, release or infrastructure automation, cloud operations, observability, and service reliability.
- Distributed teams, enterprise applications and APIs, or leading teams through ambiguity and rapid technology change.

## Success measures

Success includes consistent, high-quality delivery; stronger team capability; effective use of AI tools without weakening engineering standards; improved build-to-support practices; clear execution amid changing priorities; and growth in team performance and leadership scope.
