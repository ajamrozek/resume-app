About The Princeton Review

The Princeton Review is a leading tutoring, test prep, and college admission services company. Every year, it helps millions of college- and graduate school–bound students achieve their education and career goals through online and in-person courses delivered by a network of more than 4,000 teachers and tutors, online resources, and its more than 150 print and digital books published by Penguin Random House. The company's Tutor.com brand is one of the largest online tutoring services in the U.S. It comprises a community of thousands of tutors who have delivered more than 22 million one-to-one tutoring sessions. The Princeton Review is headquartered in New York, NY. Follow the company on LinkedIn, YouTube and Instagram.


About the Position

We're modernizing how engineering works at The Princeton Review, and we need a Senior Engineering Manager to help lead that change from the inside. Our Director of Engineering currently has roughly 15 engineers reporting to him directly, too many for anyone to coach, develop, or unlock effectively.

We're looking for someone who has personally operated inside and helped build a modern engineering organization: real CI/CD, AI tools that make engineers faster without making them worse, engineers who own their work from conception through production support, tight collaboration with product, and ceremonies that actually drive continuous improvement rather than checking a box. You'll bring that operating model here, partner with the Director of Engineering and VP of Engineering to rebuild the org around it, and coach engineers to build those muscles themselves.

Career Path: Engineering | Career Level: Senior | Career Option: People Manager

Reporting Structure:

This position reports to the VP Engineering.
This position will initially manage up to 8 engineers with a mandate to help stand up the org's first layer of engineering management over the next two quarters.

Contribute hands-on to code, architecture, and technical design while leading your team (target 40/60 IC/management split, adjusting as the team matures)
Help design and implement modern engineering practices: CI/CD, automated testing, code review, feature flagging, and static code analysis
Champion effective, responsible adoption of AI-assisted development. Tools that raise the ceiling on engineer output and code quality, not shortcuts that create new technical debt
Team Development & Change Management
Build and develop a high-performing team through effective coaching, feedback, and career development, including engineers new to modern SDLC practices
Support the implementation of clear expectations, ceremonies, and standards. Bring engineers along with the "why," not top-down mandates
As part of a small engineering leadership team, foster a culture of psychological safety, collaboration, and continuous learning
Mentor engineers as they move from reactive, deadline-driven delivery to sustainable ownership of their systems end to end
Delivery & Collaboration
Support your reports delivery from conception through production support while meeting quarterly deadlines
Build software that's scalable and maintainable by design, and delightful for the people using it
Partner with Product and Design to translate business goals into clear technical plans and ensure consistent execution
Communicate effectively across technical and non-technical audiences, ensuring alignment and managing dependencies
Drive measurable improvements in engineering KPIs
